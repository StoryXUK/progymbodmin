Exciting New Additions at ProGym Bodmin

At ProGym Bodmin, we’re always looking for ways to improve our facilities and give our members even more reasons to love their gym. We’re excited to announce that our brand-new cardio room is now open, offering more space, equipment and variety to support your fitness goals.

Whether you’re looking to improve your cardiovascular fitness, build endurance, warm up before a weights session or simply mix up your training, our new cardio room has something for everyone, with a range of equipment to suit different fitness levels and goals.

We’re also putting the finishing touches on our brand-new posing room, which will offer much more than just a space for posing practice and progress photos. It will provide additional floor space for stretching, abs and other floor-based exercises, as well as a dedicated room for our blood testing services, helping us offer even more to our members.

As a family-run, independent gym, we’re passionate about creating a friendly, welcoming environment where everyone feels comfortable, whatever their fitness level or goals. We’re continually investing in ProGym to improve our facilities while keeping the relaxed, non-commercial atmosphere that makes us who we are.

ProGym is more than just a gym. It’s a place to work towards your goals, improve your physical and mental wellbeing, and be part of a supportive community all year round.

If you haven’t visited us yet, why not get in touch and come and have a look around? We’d love to welcome you to ProGym Bodmin!
