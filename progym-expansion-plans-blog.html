ProGym Expansion Plans: Exciting Changes Coming Soon

At ProGym, we're always looking for ways to improve the training experience for our members. As some of you may have heard on our opening day, we're excited to share that expansion plans are officially underway.

Since day one, our focus has been on providing a serious strength training environment. As a result, dedicated cardio space has always been limited. We're now taking the next step in our development by creating a purpose-built cardio area designed to better serve all aspects of your training.

Our proposed new cardio space will feature a SkiErg, rowing machine, and will house our existing bikes and treadmills. We're also planning to introduce a stair climber — a highly requested addition that we're sure many members will be pleased to see arrive.

The expansion doesn't just benefit cardio users. By relocating equipment into the new area, we'll create valuable additional space within the main gym, allowing us to introduce:

  • A dedicated posing room
  • A treatment room available for sports massage therapists
  • Additional strength training equipment

High on our members' wish list is an abductor/adductor machine, and we're aiming to make that one of the next additions to the gym floor.

If everything progresses as planned, we hope to have the new area ready within the next month.

Our goal has always been simple: to continue investing in ProGym and provide the best possible training environment for our members. This expansion is another exciting step forward, and we can't wait to share it with you.

Thank you for your continued support, and don't forget to spread the word — ProGym is about to get even better.
