ProGym Bodmin Memberships: Flexible Fitness Options in Bodmin, Cornwall

If you’re looking for a friendly, well-equipped gym in Bodmin, Cornwall, ProGym Bodmin offers a range of flexible membership options designed to suit every lifestyle. Whether you’re just starting your fitness journey, visiting the area, or looking for a long-term training home, we’ve got a membership to match your goals.

Find the Right Membership for You

At ProGym Bodmin, we understand that everyone’s fitness journey is different. That’s why we offer a variety of membership plans, giving you the freedom to choose what works best for your schedule, budget, and commitment level.

New Couples Membership

Our newest and most exciting option is the Couples Membership, designed exclusively for couples who want to train together and stay motivated. Working out as a couple can help you stay accountable, make sessions more enjoyable, and keep you both progressing towards your fitness goals.

If you’re based in Bodmin or the surrounding Cornwall area, this is a fantastic way to prioritise your health together.

12-Month Membership

For those fully committed to their fitness journey, our 12-month membership offers the best long-term value. Ideal for anyone serious about building strength, improving endurance, or maintaining a consistent routine, this option provides full access to our facilities all year round.

6-Month Membership

Our 6-month membership is a great middle-ground option. It’s perfect if you’re looking for a longer-term commitment without locking in for a full year. Many of our Bodmin members choose this plan to stay focused and build lasting habits.

3-Month Membership

If you’re just getting started or prefer a shorter commitment, our 3-month membership is a popular choice. It’s a great way to kickstart your fitness journey, try out the gym, and see real progress in a manageable timeframe.

Week Passes

Visiting Bodmin or in Cornwall for a short stay? Our week passes give you full access to ProGym Bodmin without the need for a long-term commitment. Perfect for holidaymakers or those temporarily in the area who still want to stay on track with their fitness.

Day Passes

Need a one-off session? Our day passes are ideal for anyone who wants to drop in and experience the gym. Whether you’re trying us out for the first time or just need a quick workout while in Bodmin, this flexible option has you covered.

Why Choose ProGym Bodmin?

ProGym Bodmin is proud to support the local Bodmin and Cornwall community with a welcoming environment and high-quality equipment. Our memberships are designed to be flexible, accessible, and suited to all fitness levels—from beginners to experienced lifters.
